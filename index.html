Before you get started

Frequently asked questions

Do you buy signed and rare Funko Pops?

Yes, we're interested in signed and rare figures. Please include clear photographs and details of any certificates or third-party authentication you have.

Do I have to accept an offer?

No. Sending an enquiry is not a commitment to sell. Any offer will be discussed with you before you decide.

Do you buy collections outside your local area?

Yes, we welcome enquiries from across the UK. Postage, courier or collection arrangements will be discussed individually if we decide to proceed.

Will I get an automatic valuation?

No. Each collection is reviewed manually. We'll use the details and photographs you submit to decide whether we can make an offer.

Ready to sell your collection?

Send us a few details and photographs. We'll review your enquiry and get back to you if we're interested.
