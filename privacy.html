Privacy Notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

Pop & Lock operates a UK-wide service for people who want to sell Funko Pop collections. For privacy enquiries, email popandlock31@gmail.com.

Information you may provide

When you submit a collection enquiry, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, postcode, details about your collection, preferred handover method, and photographs you choose to upload. Please do not send payment details, identity documents or other information that is not needed to assess your collection.

How we use it

  • To review your collection and decide whether we may wish to make an offer.
  • To contact you about your enquiry and agree any next steps if both sides are interested.
  • To maintain reasonable records of enquiries and help prevent misuse of the form.

Submitting an enquiry does not commit you to selling and does not automatically generate a valuation.

Form service and photographs

The collection form is configured to use FormSubmit to deliver enquiries to our contact email. That means information submitted through the form, including any supported attachments, may be processed by that service. Please review the service's own privacy information before submitting. Photo upload availability and size limits depend on the form provider's current service and configuration; if upload fails, email us first and ask how to send photographs safely.

Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell enquiry information. We may use service providers needed to receive or store enquiries, or disclose information where required by law. We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, maintain appropriate business records, or meet legal obligations, then delete it or securely dispose of it where appropriate.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable data-protection law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email popandlock31@gmail.com. You can choose not to submit the form; you may still contact us by email.

Security and updates

Please share only information needed to assess your collection. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this notice when our process or legal obligations change; the date at the top will be updated when we do.
